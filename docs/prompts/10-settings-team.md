# Phase 10: Settings and team

Phase 10. Settings (/settings, docs/screens/shot-settings.png). Owner only;
staff see a read only view.

- Business profile card: name, logo upload (saved to the connected storage), phone, email, address
- Quotes card: default tax %, currency, next quote number, default footer
- Team card: invite staff by email, role Owner / Staff, remove, avatar color;
  staff can do everything except Settings, delete customers and delete
  transactions
- Storage card (Owner only): Connect Google Drive or Connect Dropbox, show the
  connected account and the JobDesk folder with an Open link, Disconnect,
  and Switch provider (offers to copy existing photos across in the background)
- Appearance: Match device / Light / Dark (dark tokens in globals.css)
- Notifications toggles: job reminders the day before, payment alerts
- Data: export customers, jobs, transactions as CSV; clear sample data
- Enforce the role rules in server actions, not only in the UI
