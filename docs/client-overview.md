## Welcome

Hi Dylan, this is a quick look at the system we're building to keep your business organized.
Your customers, jobs, job site photos, quotes and money all live in one simple place. It works
on your computer at the office and on your phone at the job site.

**You asked for four things, and here's where you'll find each one:**

| What you asked for | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| A customer and job dashboard | Your home screen and each customer's page |
| Job site photos saved to each customer | The Photos tab on every customer's page |
| Bookkeeping for money in and money out | The Books screen |
| Fast quotes from a ready list of items | The Quotes screen |

The screens below show how it will look. The names and numbers in them are only examples.

## Your home screen

This is the first thing you see each day. It shows what needs your attention at a glance.
(`docs/screens/shot-dashboard.png`)

- **Ongoing jobs** appear as colored cards, so you can see how far along each job is and how
  many days are left.
- **This month** shows the money you brought in, what you spent and what you kept.
- **Calendar** shows your scheduled jobs by day.
- **Activity** keeps a running list of what's happened, like a quote being accepted, a photo
  being added or a payment coming in.
- **Quick buttons** let you add a customer, a job, a quote or an expense in one tap.

## Your customers and jobs

Every customer gets a card, and every card opens into their own page with everything about them
in one spot. (`docs/screens/shot-customers.png`)

- **Search** by name, phone number or address.
- **Each card** shows the customer's latest job and where it stands.
- **Call, text or get directions** straight from the card.

Opening a customer shows their full page (`docs/screens/shot-profile.png`):

- **Their jobs** appear as colored cards, from new lead to completed.
- **Their details** include phone, email, address and handy notes like a gate code or a dog on
  site.
- **Their schedule** shows upcoming visits on a calendar.
- **Notes and messages** keep a record of what was said and done.
- **Tabs along the bottom** let you jump to their photos, quotes and payments.

## Job site photos

Take photos at the job and they're saved right to that customer, so you always have a record of
the work. (`docs/screens/shot-photos.png`)

- **Upload from your phone** by snapping a picture or choosing from your gallery. Several at
  once is fine.
- **Tag each photo** as Before, During or After.
- **Photos are grouped by job**, so it's easy to show a customer the finished work.
- **Add a caption** to any photo, like "Patch set, ready for paint."

## Fast quotes

Build a quote in about a minute: pick the items, type your price, and you're done.
(`docs/screens/shot-quotes.png`)

- **See every quote** and whether it's a draft, sent, accepted or paid.
- **Know what's owed** at a glance.

Making a new quote looks like this (`docs/screens/shot-builder.png`):

- **Pick from your item list** on the left, sorted into groups like TV Mounting, Electrical and
  Construction Supplies.
- **Type your own price** for each item, every time. The system shows your last price as a
  reminder.
- **Add anything else** with a custom line.
- **Tax and totals** are worked out for you.
- **Print it or save it as a PDF** to send to your customer.
- **Once the customer says yes,** one tap turns the quote into a job on your schedule.

## Bookkeeping

One simple list of the money coming in and going out, so you always know where you stand.
(`docs/screens/shot-books.png`)

- **Record payments** from customers, including deposits.
- **Log expenses** like materials, fuel, tools and insurance.
- **See your totals** for the month: money in, money out and what you kept.
- **See where your money goes** with a simple breakdown by category.
- **Payments connect to quotes,** so you can see who has paid in full and who still owes.

## Your item list

This is the ready-made list of services and supplies you pick from when making a quote. No
prices are stored here, because you set the price on each quote.
(`docs/screens/shot-catalog.png`)

- **Items are sorted into groups** like TV Mounting, Plumbing, Painting and Construction
  Supplies.
- **Add, rename or remove items** whenever you like.
- **Bring in your own list from Excel** by uploading the file or pasting it in.

**What we need from you:** an Excel sheet of the items you'd like to see. Three columns are all
it takes:

| Category | Item | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TV Mounting | TV Wall Mount | each |
| Construction Supplies | Drywall Sheets | sheet |
| Labor | Hourly Labor | hour |

## Settings and next steps

Settings is where you make the system your own. (`docs/screens/shot-settings.png`)

- **Your business details**, such as name, logo, phone and email, appear on every quote you
  send.
- **Your tax rate** is filled in on quotes automatically.
- **Your team** can be added so jobs show who's working on them.
- **Light or dark mode**, plus reminders before a visit.

**On your phone:** everything shown here works on your phone too. The screens stack neatly, and
uploading a photo opens your camera straight away.

**To get started, we'd love a few things from you:**

1. Your item list in Excel, as described above.
2. Your business name and logo for your quotes.
3. The tax rate you charge.
4. Whether anyone else on your team will be using the system.
